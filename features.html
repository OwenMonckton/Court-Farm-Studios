Studio Facilities

Our multi-purpose venue is designed to accommodate a wide variety of activities, providing everything you need for a successful class, rehearsal, or event.

The Main Hall

A spacious, open-plan environment featuring clean, sprung-style laminate flooring perfect for movement. The room is equipped with full-length wall mirrors and fixed ballet barres to support dance and fitness routines.

  • High ceilings and bright overhead lighting
  • Extensive mirror coverage
  • Easy to clean and maintain surfaces

Ancillary Spaces

The studio includes dedicated areas outside the main floor to ensure your event runs smoothly from arrival to departure.

  • Welcoming reception counter and community notice board
  • Changing areas equipped with seating and garment racks
  • Wide corridors and secure internal doors

Access & Car Park

Set against the beautiful backdrop of the South Downs, the studio offers robust accessibility for both organisers and attendees.

  • Large private gravel car park directly outside the venue
  • Ground floor, step-free main entrance
  • Straightforward equipment load-in capabilities
